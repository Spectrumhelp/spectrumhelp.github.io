SPECTRUM SUPPORT WEBSITE

Included:
- index.html: responsive homepage
- blog.html: searchable library of all 40 supplied article titles
- support.html: independent support information page
- articles/: 40 separate article pages with related-article interlinking

Installation:
1. Extract this ZIP archive.
2. Upload the contents of the spectrum-support-website folder to your hosting public root.
3. Keep the articles folder and its HTML files together.
4. Open index.html and test the navigation and article search.

Link policy:
- No external hyperlinks are included.
- Internal navigation and related-article links are included so visitors can move through the website.
- Login and Support buttons inside article pages are intentionally non-linked buttons.
